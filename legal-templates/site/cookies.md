# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how BurnCalc ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on [https://burncalc.app](https://burncalc.app) (the "Website"). Read this together with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. **First-party cookies** are set by us; **third-party cookies** are set by other services we use (for example, analytics).

We may also use **local storage** or **session storage** in your browser to remember preferences or improve performance. In this policy, "cookies" includes comparable browser storage unless we say otherwise.

## 3. Why we use cookies

### Strictly necessary

These cookies are required for basic Website operation and security (for example, load balancing or protecting against abuse). Under many laws, these do not require consent because they are necessary to provide the service you request.

### Analytics (optional)

If enabled, analytics cookies help us understand aggregate traffic and page performance. They are not used to read your calculator inputs, which stay in your browser.

### Functional (optional)

If we add features such as remembering theme or locale, we may use cookies or local storage to persist those choices.

We do **not** use cookies to sell personal information.

## 4. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings: block, delete, or alert on new cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect Website functionality.

If we deploy a cookie consent banner in your region, you can update preferences through that tool.

For Google Analytics (if enabled), you may use Google's opt-out add-on: [https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
