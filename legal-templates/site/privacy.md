# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

## Introduction

BurnCalc ("we," "our," or "us") operates [https://burncalc.app](https://burncalc.app) (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit the Website or use our browser-based startup finance calculators (the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Website, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

## Calculator data stays on your device

BurnCalc is designed so that **the numbers you enter into calculators are processed in your browser**. We do not receive, store, or transmit your calculation inputs to our servers as part of normal calculator use. If you copy, export, or share results yourself, you control where that information goes.

## Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily send us, such as:

When you visit the Website, we or our hosting and analytics providers may automatically collect:

- **Log and device data**: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and timestamps

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookies). You can manage cookies through your browser settings.
